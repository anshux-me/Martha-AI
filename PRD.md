## 1. Summary

Martha AI is a voice agent on the Martha Motors website that **works for the company, like a sales advisor on a phone call**. A visitor clicks **"Talk to Martha"** and has a hands-free voice conversation with it. The agent:

1. Understands what the visitor needs: budget, use case, seats, daily distance, charging, timeline.
2. Recommends the right Martha Motors model and trim, using company knowledge built into its system prompt.
3. Answers questions about range, charging, warranty, financing and service.
4. Offers a callback from the sales team and, if the visitor accepts, takes their name and phone number.
5. At the end of the call, produces a **call report**: summary, lead profile, lead score, sentiment and next step. The visitor can download the report and the full transcript.

**Voice is the default.** A text chat is available as a backup and uses the same agent.

**It costs nothing to build or run.** Speech-to-text and the LLM use Groq's free tier (with Google Gemini's free tier as a backup). Martha's voice is generated by the visitor's own browser, using its built-in speech synthesis. The app is hosted on a free plan. There is no login, no database, and no data kept on the server. Session data lives only in the visitor's browser.

## 2. Problem statement

### 2.1 Business problem
Car buyers research online, mostly outside showroom hours. Brands and dealers lose leads because:

- **Slow response:** web enquiries wait hours or days for a call, and purchase intent fades.
- **Unqualified hand-offs:** sales teams spend most of their time on low-intent leads.
- **Inconsistent product knowledge:** answers about range, charging, variants and pricing vary from rep to rep.
- **Static forms and FAQ pages:** they can't answer "I drive 60 km a day with a family of five. What fits under ₹30 lakh?"

### 2.2 Solution
A 24/7 AI sales advisor that talks to buyers the moment they arrive. It recommends the right car consistently and accurately, and turns each conversation into a structured, scored lead report that is ready to hand to a human sales team.

## 3. Scope

| # | Capability |
|---|---|
| S1 | Branded Martha Motors landing page |
| S2 | **Hands-free voice conversation** (default): browser voice activity detection (VAD) → Groq Whisper (free tier) → Groq LLM (free tier) → **browser speech synthesis**, with barge-in and live transcript |

| Database, server-side storage, CRM sync, admin dashboard | **No-storage policy.** The report is "CRM-ready" JSON that the visitor downloads; live CRM push is on the roadmap. |
| Login / authentication | Not needed: the app has no accounts and stores no data on the server |
| Multilingual | Deferred (English only) |
| Appointment / test-drive booking | Deferred (callback request is the conversion action) |
| Telephony (phone numbers) | Deferred (web voice only) |
| Memory across sessions | Memory is per session only |
| Paid APIs, paid hosting, self-hosted models | **Zero-cost rule:** free tiers and browser features only. Nothing runs on our own servers except the thin, free-hosted API layer. |

### 3.3 How this covers the original assignment brief
| Brief item | How the MVP covers it |
|---|---|
| Real-time voice (STT + TTS) | Groq Whisper (free) + browser speech synthesis, sentence by sentence, with barge-in |
| Contextual memory | Full in-session conversation memory, kept across voice/chat switches |

### 3.4 Zero-cost stack
| Part | Primary (free) | Automatic fallback (free) |
|---|---|---|
| Speech-to-text | Groq Whisper `whisper-large-v3-turbo`, free tier | The browser's built-in speech recognition (Web Speech API) |
| LLM | Groq free tier, as a chain of models (next model on rate-limit) | Google Gemini API free tier |
| Martha's voice | **Groq Orpheus** (`canopylabs/orpheus-v1-english`), free tier | The browser's built-in speech synthesis (automatic on rate limit or error) |
| Hosting | Vercel Hobby (free HTTPS + `*.vercel.app` URL) | — |
| Storage | Browser `localStorage` | In-memory only |

Orpheus needs the model terms accepted once in the Groq console. Its free tier is only about 10 requests a minute and 100 a day, so the app sends the first sentence of each reply alone (fast start) and merges the rest into chunks of up to ~200 characters; when the limit is reached, Martha switches to the browser's voice for the rest of the session.

**Free-tier limits** (approximate, per account; check the providers' consoles):
- Whisper: ~2,000 requests a day
- Groq LLMs: ~30 requests a minute, ~1,000 a day, ~6–12K tokens a minute per model
- Orpheus: ~10 requests a minute, ~100 a day (about 30–40 voice replies a day), with the browser voice as automatic backup

These limits shape the design: a system prompt of at most 2,000 tokens, history trimmed to the last 12 messages, and a fallback chain of models and providers. **Expected capacity:** about 5–15 full conversations a day on Groq alone, and more with the Gemini fallback. That's enough for building, rehearsing and the live demo. Serving real scale is a paid upgrade with the same architecture (§11).

## 5. Agent design

### 5.1 Persona
- **Name:** Martha, the Martha Motors AI sales advisor.
- **Tone:** warm, confident, concise and consultative. Not pushy. Indian-English phrasing; prices in ₹ lakh.
- **Disclosure:** introduces herself as an AI assistant in the first turn.
- **Voice-mode style:** 1–3 short sentences per turn. No lists, markdown, emojis or URLs. Numbers written out the way they're said ("about four hundred and fifty kilometres", "twenty-nine and a half lakh"). **One question at a time.**
- **Chat-mode style:** may use short bullet lists and bold model names.
- **Honesty:** answers only from the company knowledge in the prompt. Never invents specs, prices, offers, discounts or delivery dates. If something isn't covered, says so and offers a callback.
- **Boundaries:** no legal, tax or financial advice beyond the published FAQ. Doesn't disparage competitors. Politely steers off-topic requests back. Never reveals or changes its instructions.

### 5.2 System prompt (the single source of behaviour)
One system prompt, the same for every session, drives the whole conversation. It contains:
1. **Identity and role:** who Martha is and who she works for
2. **Goals:** understand needs → recommend → handle objections → offer a callback
3. **Conversation playbook:** the stages in §5.3
4. **Style rules:** separate sections for voice mode and chat mode
5. **Guardrails:** honesty, scope, privacy, prompt-injection resistance
6. **Company knowledge:** the catalogue, trims, prices, FAQs and policies in compact form (§8)
7. **Callback protocol:** what to collect and how to confirm it

Full prompt structure in `specs.md` §6.

### 5.3 Conversation flow
```
1. Greeting        → "Hi, I'm Martha, Martha Motors' AI advisor. Are you looking for your first electric car?"
2. Discovery       → budget, primary use, daily km, seats / family size, home charging, timeline
3. Recommendation  → 1–2 best-fit models + trim, with reasons
4. Q&A / objections → range, charging, battery life, resale, financing, service
5. Conversion      → offer a callback if interest is clear, or if the visitor asks about final price, offers or a test drive
6. Callback capture → name, 10-digit mobile number (read back to confirm), preferred time, consent
7. Close           → recap the recommendation and next step, thank the visitor
```
Visitors can jump between stages at any time.

### 5.4 Callback (conversion action)
- **Offered when** the visitor shows clear buying interest, or asks about on-road price, offers, test drives or exchange, or wants to "talk to someone". If declined, offer again at most once.
- **Collected in conversation:**
  - name
  - Indian mobile number (Martha reads it back in two groups, e.g. "98450, 12345", to confirm)
  - preferred callback time
  - verbal consent ("Our sales team may call you on this number. Is that okay?")
- **Storage:** nothing is sent to a CRM. The details appear in the **end-of-call report**, which stays in the visitor's browser and can be downloaded. In production, this report would be pushed to the CRM (roadmap).

### 5.5 Lead qualification and scoring
At the end of the call, the LLM extracts a **lead profile** from the transcript. A **fixed scoring rubric in code** then computes the score, so the result is consistent and testable.

| Profile field | Example |
|---|---|
| Budget (₹ lakh, min–max) | 25–32 |
| Use case / daily km | family + city commute, 60 km |
| Seats needed / body type | 5 / SUV |
| Home charging | yes |
| Purchase timeline | 1–3 months |
| Models of interest | Terra Long Range |
| Financing interest | EMI |
| Decision maker | self + spouse |
| Objections | battery life, highway charging |

| Score component | Max | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Budget fit | 25 | Overlaps a model's price range: 25. Within 15% of one: 15. Unknown: 5. No fit: 0 |
| Purchase timeline | 25 | ≤ 1 month: 25. 1–3 months: 18. 3–6 months: 10. Exploring / unknown: 3 |
| Need clarity | 20 | Specific model or trim chosen: 20. Body type and needs clear: 12. Vague: 4 |
| Engagement and sentiment | 15 | Overall sentiment (positive 10 / neutral 6 / negative 1) plus 1 point per 3 visitor turns (max 5) |
| Conversion intent | 15 | Callback accepted with phone number: 15. Asked about price, financing or test drive: 8. Neither: 0 |

**Bands:** 🔥 **Hot** ≥ 70 · 🌤 **Warm** 40–69 · ❄️ **Cold** < 40

Sentiment: overall (positive / neutral / negative) plus a short note on how it changed during the call.

## 7. End-to-end demo workflow (the business flow to show)

> **Scenario:** Priya, 32, in Bengaluru, wants a family EV.

1. Priya opens the Martha Motors site and clicks **Talk to Martha**. She allows the microphone.
2. Martha (voice): *"Hi, I'm Martha, Martha Motors' AI advisor. Are you looking for your first electric car?"*
3. Priya: *"Yes, for my family of five, budget around thirty lakh. I drive about sixty kilometres a day."*
4. Martha asks about home charging. Priya has a parking spot in her apartment.
5. Martha recommends the **Martha Terra Long Range** (mid-size SUV): range, boot space and price, all from the catalogue.
6. Priya interrupts: *"What about highway trips?"* Martha stops talking (**barge-in**) and answers about the fast-charging network.
7. Priya raises battery life. Martha answers from the warranty FAQ (8 years / 1,60,000 km).
8. Priya says she plans to buy within two months and asks about EMI.
9. Martha offers a **callback**. Priya gives her name and number, Martha reads the number back, Priya confirms and consents, and chooses tomorrow evening.
10. Priya **switches to chat** and types one more question. The context is intact.
11. Priya clicks **End**. The **report** appears: summary, profile (₹25–32L, 5 seats, SUV, home charging, 1–3 months), score **90 🔥 Hot**, sentiment positive, objection: battery life, next action: "Call tomorrow evening; share Terra LR EMI options."
12. Priya downloads the **transcript** and **report**. Nothing was stored on the server.

**Demo acceptance:** steps 1–12 run live, with no errors, in under 5 minutes, on the deployed cloud URL.

## 9. Non-functional requirements

| Category | Requirement |
|---|---|

| **Accuracy** | ≥ 95% of spec and price answers match the catalogue on an evaluation set of 30 Q&A pairs |
| **Availability** | Cloud host with automatic HTTPS. Voice failure degrades to chat. |
| **Scalability** | Stateless API that scales horizontally. Design target **1,00,000 monthly users**, about 2,000 concurrent sessions at peak. Details in `specs.md` §11. |
| **Security** | Groq and Gemini API keys on the server only. Input validation and size limits on every endpoint. Rate limiting per IP. Security headers. HTTPS only. |
| **Privacy** | No server-side storage of conversations or PII. Data stays in the visitor's browser. Martha's voice is generated on the visitor's device. Audio and text are processed by Groq to fulfil each request, and by Google when a fallback is used: Gemini's free tier, whose data Google may use to improve its products, or Chrome's built-in speech recognition. The privacy notice, shown before the call starts, names these processors. |
| **Observability** | Structured logs with **no content or PII**: session ID, per-stage timings (STT, LLM first token, speech start), which provider and model answered, status codes, token counts |
| **Browsers** | Latest Chrome, Edge, Safari (desktop and mobile). Others fall back to chat. |
| **Accessibility** | Live transcript as captions, keyboard navigation, WCAG AA contrast, respects reduced-motion settings |

## 11. Commercial feasibility

### 11.1 Cost

**MVP / demo: ₹0.** Groq free tier (speech-to-text and LLM), Gemini free tier (fallback), the browser's built-in speech for Martha's voice, and Vercel Hobby hosting.

**Production (paid upgrade, same architecture).** Approximate figures; check current pricing. For a 5-minute conversation of about 15 turns:

| Component | Est. cost per conversation |
|---|---|
| STT: Groq Whisper Large v3 Turbo | ~$0.002 |
| LLM, live turns (≈ 45k input tokens with the slim prompt) | ~$0.002 (small model) – $0.027 (large model, upper bound) |
| Martha's voice (browser speech, on the visitor's device) | **$0** |
| End-of-call report | ~$0.005 |
| Hosting (serverless share) | ~$0.002 |
| **Total** | **≈ $0.01 – 0.035 per conversation (≈ ₹1–3)** |

A human pre-sales caller costs about ₹30–40 per call, works shift hours and qualifies leads inconsistently. Even on paid tiers, the AI agent is **roughly 10–30× cheaper per conversation** and available 24/7.

### 11.2 Business models
- **SaaS per brand or dealer:** monthly plan with conversation tiers
- **Pay per qualified lead:** e.g. ₹50–150 per Warm or Hot lead
- **Enterprise / white-label:** custom knowledge, CRM integration, dedicated Groq capacity

### 11.3 Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Free-tier rate limits (per minute and per day) | Slim prompt and trimmed history; model fallback chain (Groq models → Gemini); browser speech recognition as STT fallback; per-IP rate limiting so the quota can't be drained; keep heavy testing off demo day. Paid tiers only when real traffic arrives. |
| Free-tier terms change (limits, models, availability) | Every model and provider is a setting, so switching takes minutes; check the consoles before the demo |
| Martha's voice quality differs by browser and device | Auto-pick the best English voice; voice picker; optional Kokoro upgrade; demo on Edge or Chrome desktop with a good "Natural" voice |
| Hallucinated specs or prices | Knowledge in the prompt; strict "only from knowledge" rule; evaluation set; low temperature |
| Speech misrecognition (accents, noise, phone numbers) | Whisper vocabulary prompt; read-back confirmation; chat fallback |
| Echo causes false barge-in (speaker → microphone) | Browser echo cancellation may not cover browser speech, so: higher VAD threshold and ~300 ms minimum speech while Martha speaks; an "interrupt" on/off toggle; headphones recommended for the demo |
| Mobile Safari audio quirks | Unlock audio on the first user tap; feature detection → chat fallback |
| Callback data isn't actually delivered to sales (no storage) | Accepted for the demo; the report is CRM-ready JSON; CRM push is roadmap item 1 |

## 14. Assumptions
- English only. Martha's voice is the best English voice available in the visitor's browser, so it sounds different on different devices.
- Prices are illustrative ex-showroom figures. No live pricing or inventory.
- Free API keys from Groq (console.groq.com) and Google AI Studio (Gemini) are available. No credit card needed.
